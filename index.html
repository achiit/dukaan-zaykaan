About ZAYKAAN

A spice box every home trusts, now with a name

I grew up watching my mother open the masala box before every meal. The colour and smell of that box decided how the whole house ate. In 2026 I started ZAYKAAN to bring that same care to packets that anyone around Sekhuiyan Chauraha can buy.

We begin with four spices: Haldi, Mirchi, Dhania and Jeera. I want each one to taste the same every time you buy it. I would rather do four things well than ten things halfway.

My plan is simple. Earn trust at our own chauraha, then reach nearby villages, towns and districts, one shop and one kitchen at a time. Call me directly any time.

  • Consistent quality in every packet
  • Attractive, easy-to-spot packaging
  • Direct contact with the owner
  • Local retailer and distributor supply
  • Growing range, starting from your daily needs
